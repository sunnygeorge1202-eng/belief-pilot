3. Does earlier partial uptake predict later revision?

Outcome: revision at the test report (belief before minus belief after, in points). Predictors are z-scored; coefficients are points per SD. Standard errors are clustered by participant. Unresolved = gap at the challenge report minus the revision made. It splits into discounted = (1 - weight) × gap and under-learned = weight × gap - revision. Note that total unresolved is, by construction, the gap remaining at the test report; the split is what the theory adds.
